The threat

What happens when your stream is stolen

Rebroadcasting is copyright theft. It happens silently, at scale, every time you go live — and most creators never know until it is too late.

👥

Audience fragmentation

Your viewers are split across unauthorized streams. Your real viewer count drops. Your algorithm ranking suffers. Your community is divided.

💰

Revenue theft

Rebroadcasters run ads, collect Super Chats, and receive donations on your content. They profit directly from your work and your audience.

🏷

Brand damage

Viewers who find unauthorized streams may believe they are official. Poor quality, wrong branding, and misleading content damage your reputation.

⏰

Too slow to stop

By the time you manually discover a rebroadcast, thousands have already watched it. The damage is done before you can act.

Security features

Built for real-time content protection

Every feature exists to catch rebroadcasters faster and help you stop them immediately.

🎵

Audio fingerprinting

Detects your content by its audio signature. Works even when someone adds a watermark, logo, or visual overlay over your stream.

👁

Visual fingerprinting

Compares video frames using perceptual hashing. Catches rebroadcasts that change audio pitch or add background music.

⚡

60-second detection

Scans YouTube every 60 seconds while you are live. Most unauthorized streams are flagged within the first two minutes.

📲

Instant Telegram alerts

Get notified the moment a rebroadcast is confirmed. Channel name, viewer count, stream link — everything you need to act.

📋

One-click reporting

Every alert includes a direct link to file a YouTube copyright report. Business plan auto-files reports on confirmed matches.

🌐

Web dashboard

Monitor your channels and view all detected rebroadcasts from any browser or phone. Your whole team stays informed.
